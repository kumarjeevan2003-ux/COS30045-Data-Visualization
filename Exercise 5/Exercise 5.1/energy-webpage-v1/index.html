Australian Appliance Energy Consumption

Understanding how household appliances use electricity is critical for managing utility costs and reducing carbon emissions. Australia's Energy Rating Label scheme assists consumers in comparing the efficiency of various domestic products.

The Star Rating System

In Australia, appliances are graded from 1 to 10 stars. The more stars an appliance carries, the more energy-efficient it is compared to similar models on the market.
