Television Energy Ratings & Efficiency

Televisions are among the most frequently used electronics in modern Australian homes. Panel technology, screen size, and backlight configurations all influence energy demand.

Display Technology

OLED and modern Mini-LED televisions deliver high energy efficiency compared to older LCD backlights, particularly when managing black levels and power delivery per zone.

Screen Size Impact

A larger display demands significantly more power. An 85-inch television will typically consume more than double the energy of a comparable 55-inch model with the same star rating.

Eco & Power-Saving Modes

Most modern TVs come equipped with ambient light sensors and auto-dimming features to help keep consumption in check during daytime and nighttime viewing.
